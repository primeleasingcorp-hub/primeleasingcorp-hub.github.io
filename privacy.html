Privacy Policy — Muse Studio Poster

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Muse Studio Poster ("the App") is an internal tool used by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Data the App accesses

Through TikTok's OAuth authorization, the App receives:

  • Basic profile information for the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar) — used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token — used only to call the Content Posting API on the authorized account's behalf.

Data the App does not collect

The App does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. It does not read messages, contacts, browsing history, or analytics. It does not track users and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Video content

Videos published through the App are supplied by the account owner. The App transmits them to TikTok for posting and does not retain copies beyond the publishing operation.

Token storage and revocation

Tokens are held only for the operator's own use and are never shared. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's settings, which immediately ends the App's access.
